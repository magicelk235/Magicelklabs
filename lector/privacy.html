Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Lector is a menu bar app for macOS that copies and translates text from your screen. This policy explains what it captures, what it keeps, and what it sends. In short: everything happens on your Mac, and nothing you capture is kept or sent anywhere.

What Lector captures

  • Only when you ask. Lector captures the screen only when you press one of its shortcuts (⌘⇧2, ⌘⇧1 or ⌘⇧9 unless you've changed them), and it uses macOS's own screenshot tool to do it. That's why it needs the Screen Recording permission. It doesn't need Accessibility.
  • Grabs and translations. The capture goes to a private temporary file that Lector reads and deletes straight away.
  • Live translation. Lector watches only the area you drew, only until you stop it, and reads it in memory without saving anything.

How your text is read and translated

Everything happens on your Mac. Lector reads the text with Apple's Vision and a bundled copy of Tesseract, and translates it with Apple's on-device Translation or an offline Opus-MT language pack. Text Lector has translated since you opened it is kept in memory so it can show up again straight away. It's gone when Lector quits.

Nothing you capture is kept or sent anywhere, and there are no analytics.

What Lector stores

Lector stores a few things on your Mac, and never the text itself:

  • Settings, such as the language you picked for an app and the last few languages you translated between, so it can offer them first.
  • How many grabs and translations you've made this month, to apply your plan's limits. The counts start over on the first of each month.
  • Your license key, if you enter one, with the email address Gumroad has for that purchase and the date Gumroad last confirmed it. This lives in Lector's Application Support folder.
  • Language packs you've downloaded, also in Lector's Application Support folder.

What Lector sends, and where

Lector talks to three services, and only for these reasons:

  • Hugging Face, for language packs. A pack is 100 to 250 MB. Lector downloads one on its own only when it's the only way to translate a language, because Apple's Translation doesn't support it. If you turn on Download packs for instant drafts in Settings › Translation, it downloads packs for other languages too.
  • Gumroad, for your license. If you enter a license key, Lector sends that key to Gumroad to check it, when you enter it and again at each launch. A key Gumroad has confirmed keeps working for 30 days without a connection.
  • GitHub, for updates. From the second launch, Lector asks whether it may check for updates. If you allow it, Lector asks GitHub once a day whether there's a new version. You can turn this off in Settings › General.

None of these requests carries anything you captured.

Payments

The Text and Translate plans are sold through Gumroad, which processes your purchase and payment. We never see or store your card details. Gumroad's handling of your data is governed by Gumroad's privacy policy.

Removing what Lector stores

  • Settings › License removes your license key from this Mac.
  • Settings › Translation lists each language pack with its size and can remove any of them. It also lists the languages Lector remembers for each app, which you can change or remove.

Children

Lector is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.
