Questions

Does its developer make one for Firefox?

Yes: Augmented Steam for Firefox (addons.mozilla.org).

Does it stay after Firefox quits?

Yes. Firefox removes any add-on Mozilla didn't sign when it quits, so Viaduct loads yours again each time Firefox opens, with its settings. Viaduct has to stay on in Login Items for that.

Is it the real extension, or a clone?

The real one. Viaduct downloads the same package Chrome installs from the Web Store and adds fixes where SafariFirefox behaves differently.

Will it update itself?

With a license, yes. Turn on Update Chrome Web Store extensions weekly in Viaduct's settings, and it rebuilds the extension when the store has a new version.

What does Viaduct cost?

The first 2 conversions are free, and Viaduct removes them after 7 days. A Safari or Firefox license costs $19 once, converts as many as you like in that browser and keeps them installed. Mixed covers both for $29.
