Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Viaduct is a native macOS app that converts Google Chrome extensions into native Safari Web Extensions. This policy explains what it does, and does not do, with your data.

The short version

Viaduct collects no personal data. All conversion happens locally on your Mac. Nothing about the extensions you convert, and nothing about you, is sent to us. The one exception is a few anonymous usage counts that go along with your purchase when you click "Buy a license" in the app (see below).

What runs on your Mac

When you convert an extension, Viaduct reads the extension files you point it at, transforms them, and signs and installs the resulting Safari extension, all on your machine, using Apple's own developer tools (Xcode) and a bundled copy of the Node.js runtime. The extension source, the converted output, and your signing identity never leave your Mac.

When you convert an extension that is also installed in Chrome or another Chromium browser (Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi, Opera, Chromium), Viaduct reads that extension's saved settings from the browser's profile folder so it can bring them over. Import from Chrome reads the same folders to list the extensions you have. Recent versions of macOS ask for your permission first, and none of it leaves your Mac.

Data we collect

Viaduct has no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no servers that receive your data. When you click "Buy a license" inside the app, the Gumroad checkout link it opens carries the values below to Gumroad. If you complete the purchase, Gumroad passes them to us with the sale:

  • how many free conversions you used;
  • how many converted extensions you have installed;
  • how many days ago Viaduct was first set up on your Mac;
  • whether you clicked from the paywall or from Settings; and
  • your Viaduct and macOS version numbers.

That is all the link carries. No extension names, file paths, or identifiers are included, and nothing is sent unless you click that link.

Network access

Converting runs on your Mac. Viaduct reaches the network only to:

  • download its conversion engine from npm on first launch, and check npm for a newer version each time it starts;
  • check for app updates once a day, after you allow it on first launch;
  • download an extension from the Chrome Web Store when you click "Add to Safari", and, if you turn on weekly updates in Pro, check the store once a week for new versions of those extensions;
  • check your license key with Gumroad when you activate Pro and when Viaduct starts; and
  • open external links (such as this page or the store) in your browser when you click them.

Apart from the license key sent to Gumroad, these requests contain no personal information about you.

Payments

Viaduct is free for your first two conversions. Pro is sold through Gumroad, which processes your purchase and payment details. We never see or store your payment information. Gumroad's handling of your data is governed by Gumroad's privacy policy.

Third parties

Aside from Gumroad for payment, Viaduct shares no data with third parties.

This website

magicelklabs.com counts its visitors with Umami. For each visit it records the page, the site or link that sent you there, your country, language, browser and device type, and whether you clicked a Buy or Download button. Umami doesn't set cookies or store anything in your browser, and it doesn't keep your IP address. If you arrive from another site or a tagged link, the site keeps that source in your browser until you close the tab and adds it to its Gumroad links, so Gumroad can show where a sale came from. The Viaduct app itself sends none of this.

Children

Viaduct is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.
