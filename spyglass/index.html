Questions

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

How does the free trial work?

The 7 days start the first time you open Spyglass, and every format preview works during that week. You don't need a card or an account.

When the week is up, those files fall back to plain text or a short file summary with a note, until you add a license. Google Drive cards stay free either way.

How do I activate my license?

Your Gumroad receipt has a license key in it. Click the Spyglass icon in the menu bar, paste the key and click Unlock. The same key works on each of your Macs.

I bought Spyglass 1.0. What do I get?

Pro + Drive, free, for life. Your 1.0 key keeps working in 2.0 and unlocks every format preview along with the rendered Google Drive previews. If Spyglass ever asks for it, paste the key from your original receipt.

What happens if I cancel Pro + Drive?

You can cancel from your Gumroad receipt whenever you like, and everything keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, Drive files go back to the free card, and format previews need a Pro license.

Why do CSV and .txt files look the same as before?

macOS keeps those two types for its own preview and never passes them to a Quick Look extension, so no app can take them over. TSV and JSON Lines files do get Spyglass's table view.

Space still shows the old preview. What's wrong?

First, check that Spyglass is in your Applications folder and that you've opened it once. If another Quick Look app is installed, it may be handling the same file types, and macOS only uses one extension per type.

On macOS 15 or later, open System Settings, General, Login Items & Extensions, find Quick Look under Extensions and turn off the other app's extension. On macOS 14 the same list is under System Settings, Privacy & Security, Extensions, Quick Look.
